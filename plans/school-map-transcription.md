# School floor plan - transcription of the hand-drawn sketch

Read from three photographs of one pencil sketch on brown paper (one full view,
two close-ups). It is a single floor, drawn as a long band with a bent wing on
the left. Labels are copied as written; "Grade -" means the grade was left
blank on the sketch. Unlabelled shapes are described in brackets.
